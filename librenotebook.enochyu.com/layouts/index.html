LibreNotebook

A Giant Open Source Notebook

Hey there! Welcome to LibreNotebook, an open source notebook that I write as I explore various fields of interests. It currently reflects my fields of interest, but it aims to make difficult concepts more accessible by providing personal understandings and explanations that differs from traditional textbook or AI generated explanations. Yes, that's right. No LLM was used in writing and creating LibreNotebook. I don't hate AI, but I want to make sure that LibreNotebook is written by a human who is breathing on Earth.

Having said that, LibreNotebook is not meant to replace textbooks nor dedicated resources. The amount of depth and the process of publishing contents are not comparable. Rather, it is a personal appreciation towards open education and an effort to inspire others by returning skills and knowledge that I received from the public. Ultimately, LibreNotebook is a passion towards learning, open education, and the sharing of knowledge. Hope you enjoy it!
