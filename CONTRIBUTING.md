# Contributing to LibreNotebook

First and foremost, thank you so much for your interest and taking
your time to contribute! All contributions, really any contributions
ranging from a typo to writing a new note, are welcomed and
encouraged with minor details below. To contribute, please either open
an issue or submit a pull request.

## No Generative AI

I don't hate AI! Indeed, I have many other projects where I actively
use LLMs for assistance. Whereas, I have projects like this one where
I have everything human-written as that is one component that makes
them special.

*LibreNotebook* so far is entirely written by hand, and I believe
future contributions should also respect that as well. Please do not
submit any contributions that has been entirely written or polished
by AI. If, however the contents include AI generated contents,
please disclose it for transparency of contributors :)
Ordinary AI tools like...
  * Spell checks for original contents
  * Checking whether the content is correct
  * Debugging LaTeX compilation and Tikz errors

are fine, but we cannot accepts AI generated contributions like...
  * Using AI to generate contents and paraphrasing afterwards
  * Using AI as a "search engine" as a sole way to find contents
    like definitions and theorems to include in the notes
  * Using AI to rewrite a large portion of the original contents


Please be kind and welcoming, and thank you for your time and efforts!
